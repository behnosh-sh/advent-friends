How to play
  1. Drag your finger anywhere on the maze (or use the arrow keys). The gingerbread man walks the same way, but not through walls.
  2. Reach the present when exactly 15 seconds have passed. Each maze has a longer path than the one before, so in the last one you’ll need to hurry.
  3. The clock starts when you begin walking. It shows for the first 3 seconds and then disappears. You have to count in your head.
  4. It’s fine to stand and wait next to the present. The clock stops the moment you step onto the present.
  5. After each maze you see how far off you were. The errors are added up, and the lowest total wins. The medal limits are shown above the maze.

Everyone gets the same mazes. Only your first attempt counts. The clock keeps running even if you close the page in the middle of a maze.
