How to play
  1. Tap Play. Today’s five pictures are the same for everyone (no account needed).
  2. For each picture, guess the year and drop a pin on the map. The closer you are, the more points, up to 10,000 per picture.
  3. When the game is over, tap Share, come back here and paste the text.

Most points out of 50,000 wins; a tie means a shared place.
Medals: gold from 40,000, silver from 32,000, bronze from 25,000.
TimeGuessr switches to new pictures at midnight UK time.
