Nerdle

🧮

Like Wordle, but with math. Guess today’s equation in six tries or fewer. Fewest guesses wins.

Only your first result counts.

How to play
  1. Tap Play. Today’s Nerdle opens (the classic one with 8 boxes, no account needed). Everyone gets the same equation today.
  2. Type an equation that’s correct, for example 12+35=47. Green is the right character in the right spot, purple is in the equation but in the wrong spot, black isn’t in it at all.
  3. When the game is over, tap Share, come back here and paste the text.

Fewest guesses wins; a tie means a shared place. If you don’t solve it in six tries, you end up last among those who played.
Medals: gold for 1 to 3 guesses, silver for 4, bronze for 5.
Nerdle switches to a new equation at midnight.
