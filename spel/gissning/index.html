    How to play
    1. Guess a number for each question. No time limit, but no googling!
    2. After each guess you see the correct answer and how close you got, from green to red.
    3. What counts is how many times off you are, so big and small answers weigh the same. Half or double is equally wrong.
    4. Each question gives 0–100 points, shown at the end. Highest total wins. Gold from 400, silver from 330, bronze from 250.

    Only your first try at today’s questions counts.
