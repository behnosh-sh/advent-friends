How to play
  1. Tap Play. Globle opens (no account needed). Everyone gets the same country today.
  2. Type in a country. It’s colored by how close it is to the secret country: yellow is far away, dark red is very close.
  3. When you’ve found the country, tap Share, come back here and paste the text.

Medals: gold for 1 to 5 guesses, silver for 6 to 10, bronze for 11 to 15.
