How to play
  1. A crossword without clues. Every square has a number, and the same number is always the same letter. All words are common English words, across and down.
  2. Three letters are filled in from the start. Tap a square and pick the letter you think the number stands for. It gets filled in everywhere that number appears.
  3. Not every letter from A to Z has to be used. The table below the grid shows the numbers in play.
  4. You won’t be told if a letter is wrong. When everything is right, the puzzle is solved.
  5. If you get stuck, you can take a hint. It reveals a correct letter but costs 30 seconds.
  6. The clock starts when you tap Start. Lowest time wins. Gold under 5 minutes, silver under 8, bronze under 12.

Only your first try counts. The clock keeps running even if you close the page.
