How to play
  1. Tap Play. Today’s Wikitrivia opens (no account needed). Everyone gets the same cards today.
  2. Drag each new card to the right spot on the timeline. A wrong spot costs one of your three lives.
  3. When the game is over, tap Share, come back here and paste the text.

Most cards placed correctly wins; a tie means a shared place.
Medals: gold for at least 20, silver for at least 12, bronze for at least 6.
Wikitrivia switches to new cards at midnight UTC.
