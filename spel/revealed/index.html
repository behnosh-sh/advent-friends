How to play
  1. Tap Play. Revealed opens on Britannica (no account needed). Everyone gets the same text today.
  2. Reveal words one at a time or take a hint, and guess the topic when you think you know it.
  3. When you’ve solved it, tap Share, come back here and paste the text.

Score = revealed words + hints, lowest wins. In a tie, whoever revealed fewer words wins.
Medals: gold for 0 to 3 points, silver for 4 to 7, bronze for 8 to 12.
