How to play
  1. You get five series, one at a time: numbers, letters or shapes. The last one is a 3×3 grid of shapes where the bottom right one is missing.
  2. Find the pattern and tap the one of the four options that comes next. You get one try per series.
  3. After each answer you see whether you were right and what the pattern was. The clock stops while you read.
  4. Most correct wins. In a tie, the fastest wins. Gold for 5 correct, silver for 4, bronze for 3.

Only your first try at today’s series counts.
